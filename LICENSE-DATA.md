## Personal data

**No personal data is released.** Specifically excluded:

- Raw review text
- Reviewer names, display names, profile identifiers
- Reviewer profile images
- Any record traceable to an individual reviewer

**What is released:** Outlet-level aggregation only—mean polarity per aspect, mean rating over the most recent twelve months, temporal sentiment change per aspect, and review count. No analysis is conducted at the individual reviewer level.

The data is provided **as is**, without warranty of any kind.

- The suitability score is a relative spatial index derived from historical outlet patterns
- It is **not** a probability, not a valuation, and not a prediction of commercial success
- The authors and Bina Nusantara University accept **no liability** for commercial decisions made on the basis of this material

Any real siting decision requires:
- Field survey
- Parcel availability and legal status checks
- Zoning and permit verification
- Rent negotiation
- Access and visibility assessment
- Flood-risk and logistics review
- Financial feasibility analysis

None of these are modeled here.
